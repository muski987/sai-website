For You ❤️

I didn’t plan you, not like people plan love stories, no perfect timing, no perfect version of me. You just happened, and suddenly everything felt a little more real. Somewhere between random conversations and moments I didn’t even notice then, you became… my favourite part of everything. It’s funny, how you act like you don’t care sometimes, and yet somehow, you’re the only place I feel completely held. I don’t say it all out loud, not always, but there are things I feel every time I’m with you, that words don’t really do justice to. So I didn’t try to give you something perfect, I just gave you pieces of me instead, for the 19 years I didn’t get to love you. And maybe I can’t go back and be there then, but I’m here now… and I don’t plan on leaving anytime soon.
